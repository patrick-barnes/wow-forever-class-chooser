# Fix: "Cleave" is iconic for Warriors

**Cleave is iconic for Warriors**, but there are two meanings worth separating.

**The ability `Cleave`** is absolutely an iconic Warrior ability. It dates back to early WoW and embodies the Warrior fantasy: swing a weapon and hit an additional nearby enemy. Alongside abilities such as Charge, Execute, Heroic Strike, and the stance system, it's one of the recognizable old-school Warrior mechanics.

**"Cleave" as a type of damage** is also particularly characteristic of Warriors in WoW Forever. This connects directly to Blizzard's intended class distinction:

- **Rogue:** premier sustained **single-target** melee damage.
- **Warrior:** particularly strong sustained **multi-target melee/cleave** damage.

So if you're trying to reduce each class to a short "signature strength," I'd characterize Warrior as:

> **Warrior — sustained melee cleave, weapon mastery, and physical toughness.**

There's an important distinction from **AoE**, though. Cleave generally means damaging your primary target **plus one or a few nearby targets**, whereas AoE means damaging a larger group. A Warrior being the iconic cleave class therefore doesn't necessarily mean Blizzard intends Warrior to have the game's best large-pack AoE.

In fact, **"cleave" is a better shorthand for the Forever Warrior identity than "multi-target damage"**, because it captures the particular melee/weapon-based way Warriors excel at fighting several enemies at once.
