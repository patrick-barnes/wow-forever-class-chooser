# Classes that can heal

In **WoW Forever**, four classes can function as full healers:

| Class | Healing specialization | Healing identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Priest** | **Holy** | Broad, versatile traditional healing |
| **Priest** | **Discipline** | Shields/prevention + direct healing |
| **Paladin** | **Holy** | Exceptional **single-target** healing |
| **Shaman** | **Restoration** | **Chain/group healing** + totem support |
| **Druid** | **Restoration** | **Healing-over-time (HoT)** specialist |

A few distinctions matter.

**Priest actually has two healing-oriented specs.** Holy is the conventional dedicated healer, while Discipline is a legitimate healing/support build centered more heavily around Power Word: Shield and absorbs. Forever strengthens that identity with **Soul Warding, Penance, Renewed Hope, and Divine Aegis**. [World of Warcraft](https://wow-site-bwa-production-eks-prod-use1-01.worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301514?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Holy Paladin** is the focused-healing specialist. Blizzard is deliberately preserving its strength in **single-target healing**, while Forever gives it some additional group-healing capability through Light's Vigil rather than turning it into another Priest. [World of Warcraft](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Restoration Shaman** is especially distinctive for healing multiple people through **Chain Heal**, while also contributing healing and support through totems. Forever adds Riptide, which specifically makes subsequent Chain Heals on that target 25% more effective. [World of Warcraft](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-mage-and-shaman?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Restoration Druid** remains the HoT-oriented healer—Renew/Rejuvenation-style sustained healing and mobility rather than Priest-like reactive healing.

So, analogous to our earlier tank discussion:

**Full tank specs:** Protection Warrior, Protection Paladin, Feral Bear Druid  
**Full healing specs:** Holy Priest, Discipline Priest, Holy Paladin, Restoration Shaman, Restoration Druid

There is one interesting wrinkle: **non-healing specs of these four classes still retain healing spells.** For example, an Enhancement Shaman can cast Healing Wave, and a Feral Druid can shift out and heal. That's part of Forever's Classic-style class identity. But that doesn't make Enhancement or Feral *healer specs*.

And this gives us another useful set of "iconic niches":

**Paladin → single-target healing**  
**Priest → versatile healing / absorbs**  
**Shaman → chain/group healing + totems**  
**Druid → healing over time**

Those distinctions appear quite intentional in Forever rather than merely being differences in rotation.
