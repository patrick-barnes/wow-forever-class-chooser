# Classes that can tank

## Classes intended to tank

In **WoW Forever**, there are **three intended tank classes/specs**:

| Class | Tank specialization | Tank style |
|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | **Protection** | Traditional sword-and-shield tank; strong mitigation and defensive toolkit |
| **Paladin** | **Protection** | Holy/magic tank; blocking, absorbs, Consecration, excellent group utility |
| **Druid** | **Feral Combat — Bear Form** | High armor/health, strong threat; Feral also encompasses Cat DPS |

Blizzard explicitly designed all three as viable tanks. Protection Paladin in particular received substantial Forever changes—including **Seal of Fury**, whose Judgment acts as a 10-yard taunt, and baseline Consecration for multi-target threat. [World of Warcraft](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24303313?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Shaman

This is the interesting edge case. **Enhancement Shaman can tank to some degree**, particularly while leveling and in less demanding content, and some Forever guides categorize it as an emergency/off-meta tank. [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/classes?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

But Blizzard has **not positioned Enhancement as a fourth proper endgame tank specialization**. The intended tank roster is Warrior, Paladin, and Druid. [Endgame Tools](https://endgametools.com/en/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-classes?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So I would distinguish them as:

**Official/full tanks:**  
**Protection Warrior • Protection Paladin • Feral Bear Druid**

**Can situationally tank but isn't intended as a full tank spec:**  
**Enhancement Shaman**

One unusual thing about Forever is **Feral Druid**: unlike later versions of WoW where Guardian became a separate specialization, Forever retains the Classic model where **Feral Combat contains both Cat DPS and Bear tanking**. That makes Feral the only specialization that is deliberately both a **DPS and tank spec**. [icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/all-class-guides/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
